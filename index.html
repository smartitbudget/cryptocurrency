⛏️ How new cryptocurrency coins are created

A calm, step-by-step look at Bitcoin-style “mining”.

READY

Press “Start mining” to begin

You will see how payments become a block, how a miner competes to add it, and where brand-new coins come from.

🪙 Where new coins come from

  • Only the miner who solves the puzzle first is rewarded
  • The reward is brand-new coins plus the fees people paid
  • Bitcoin’s reward halves about every four years
  • The total supply is capped, so creation slows down

🔍 Good to know

  • Guessing is the “work”: it takes real electricity
  • Checking an answer is instant, so anyone can verify
  • Changing an old block would mean redoing all the work after it
  • Some coins use staking instead of mining and need far less energy
